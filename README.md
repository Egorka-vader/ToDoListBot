## ✅ TodoList Bot     

Bot for managing tasks, reminders, and daily to-do lists — works in Telegram, CLI, or as a web widget.    

### Features    

📝 **Create & organize tasks** — add title, description, due date, priority    
⏰ **Smart reminders** — notifications via Telegram, email, or push   
🏷️ **Tags & categories** — work, personal, shopping, urgent   
🔁 **Recurring tasks** — daily, weekly, or custom intervals   
📊 **Progress tracking** — completed vs pending + weekly stats    
🔍 **Search & filters** — by date, tag, status, or keyword   
📎 **Share lists** — export to PDF/TXT or collaborate with team   
🎨 **Minimal & fast** — works without registration (optional auth)
